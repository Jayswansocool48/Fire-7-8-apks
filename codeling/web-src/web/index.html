Privacy, terms & your data

Privacy policy

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app stores only your learning progress (completed lessons, boss scores, XP, streak dates, achievements and your daily goal) and the files you create in the code editor. In the desktop app and the web app, progress stays on your device. When you open Codeling inside Claude while signed in, it's also saved in private per-person storage so it follows you across devices; nobody else can read it, including the person who shared the page. The AI tutor is off until you set it up. Inside Claude, the tutor is Claude: it sends the lesson, question or code you're working on to Claude when you press a tutor button, and nothing otherwise. In the desktop app and the web app, the tutor runs an AI model on your own computer with Ollama, so what you send it stays on your computer. Lesson steps you've reached and practice questions the tutor wrote are saved on your device.

Cookies

This app sets no cookies, so there is no cookie banner. Progress uses your browser's local storage and your private saved progress, never advertising cookies.

Third parties

Fonts load from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads. The first time you run Python in the browser, Python is downloaded from the jsDelivr CDN, which also receives your IP address. The desktop app runs code with the compilers installed on your own computer, and only contacts GitHub when you press Check for updates. If you choose to set up the AI tutor, the app downloads Ollama's installer from ollama.com and AI models from Ollama's model library, only when you press those buttons; Ollama then runs on your computer under its own terms. Sound effects are generated by the app itself; no audio is downloaded. No other outside services are used. Links to official docs open those sites in a new tab under their own policies.

Terms of use

The lessons are free for personal learning and provided as-is. Engines and APIs change, so check the linked official docs when something doesn't match.

Price & refunds

Everything here is free. XP, levels and achievements have no cash value, and there are no purchases, subscriptions, or hidden fees, so there is nothing to refund.

Younger learners

No names, emails, or other personal details are collected or shown, only lesson progress. There are no chats, leaderboards, or ways to contact other people.

Delete your data

Erase all your progress, XP, streak, achievements and code files from this device (and your synced copy, when you're using Codeling inside Claude). This can't be undone. To remove the whole app as well, use Uninstall in Settings (the gear at the top).

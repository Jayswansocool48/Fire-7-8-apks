Privacy, terms & your data

Privacy policy

Codeling has no ads, analytics or tracking. It stores your lesson progress (finished lessons, boss scores, XP, streak dates, achievements and daily goal), the files you make in the code editor, and your settings. In the desktop app, the tablet app and the web app, all of it stays on your device. If you open Codeling inside Claude while signed in, Claude also keeps your progress in private storage tied to your account, so it follows you to your other devices. Nobody else can read it, including the person who shared the page.

The AI tutor stays off until you set it up. Inside Claude, the tutor is Claude: after you agree, each press of a tutor button sends Anthropic the lesson, question or code on your screen, and nothing else. You can turn this off in Settings → AI tutor. Everywhere else, the tutor is an AI model that runs on your own computer through Ollama, so your questions stay on your computer and your home Wi-Fi.

Cookies

Codeling sets no cookies, so you see no cookie banner. It saves your progress in your browser's local storage or the app's own storage.

Third parties

Codeling carries its own fonts and sounds, so opening it contacts no outside server. The app reaches the internet only when you start one of these:

  • Check for updates asks GitHub for the newest version.
  • Running Python in the browser downloads Python from jsDelivr after you agree. jsDelivr receives your IP address.
  • Install Ollama and model downloads in the AI tutor settings contact ollama.com and Ollama's model library.
  • Links to official docs open those sites in your browser, under their own policies.

Terms of use

You can use the lessons free for personal learning. Codeling comes as-is, with no warranty. Engines and APIs change, so check the linked official docs when a lesson doesn't match what you see. Code you run in the desktop app runs on your own computer, the same as code you run in a terminal.

Price & refunds

Codeling is free. It has no purchases, subscriptions or hidden fees, so you have nothing to refund. XP, levels and achievements have no cash value.

Younger learners

Codeling collects no names, emails or other personal details from anyone, at any age. It has no chat, no leaderboards and no way to contact other people.

Who makes Codeling

Jayswansocool48, an independent developer, makes and publishes Codeling. To report a problem or ask about your data, open an issue at github.com/Jayswansocool48/codeling/issues.

Delete your data

Delete my progress erases your progress, XP, streak, achievements and code files from this device, plus your synced copy when you use Codeling inside Claude. You get 8 seconds to press Undo. To remove the app as well, use Uninstall in Settings (the gear at the top).

Keyboard: Ctrl K searches every lesson and action. Tab moves, Enter or Space selects. In a lesson, ← and → move between lessons. In the editor, Ctrl Enter runs, Ctrl / comments a line, Ctrl S saves, and Esc then Tab moves focus out of the code. Ctrl Z undoes a delete while its Undo button shows.

Privacy and terms

Remove Hub

This deletes everything Hub keeps on the server: settings, notes, drawings, wallpapers, caches, files sent to the TV, and the Hub server files. Your media folders stay as they are.

To take Hub off the TV too, type the TV's developer password. Leave it blank to keep the channel.
